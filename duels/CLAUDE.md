# duels module

`Duels` wires everything (the `JavaPlugin` only starts and stops it, so tests run it on a mock plugin). Packages: `arena` (YAML registry; arenas have categories that kits pick from, an optional center and build limit; `ArenaInstances` lends arenas to duels, one at a time, puts back blocks changed by build kits from `ArenaChanges`, and rebuilds arenas a crash left changed from `ArenaTemplate` snapshots (`.blocks`); while an arena is busy it leases a copy from `ArenaPool`, which pastes the arena's WorldEdit snapshot (`arenas/<arena>.schem`, saved with the `.blocks` one by `/duels arena snapshot`) on a grid in the void `ArenaWorld`. Copies live only in memory, are `Arena` records with the arena's name and `Arena.copy` set (so history, rematches and placeholders see the arena), and the `ArenaInstance` of a fresh copy completes `ready()` once pasted: `MatchManager.start` keeps the fighters in `STARTING` until both their snapshot save and the paste are done, so callers stay synchronous. `ArenaPool.maintain` (from `ArenaInstances.tick`, every second) pastes one ahead while an arena has fewer than `arenas.pool.warm` free places (the arena itself counts while free) and clears copies free for `idle-timeout` beyond those or no longer matching their arena record or snapshot; pasted boxes are recorded in arenas.yml `pool-boxes` and cleared at the next start. Old `copy-of` entries from `/duels arena pregen` are dropped and their boxes cleared by `ArenaRegistry`), `kit` (YAML registry; `KitRule` per-kit game rules enforced in `CombatListener` (`build` and `damage` among them), unset ones taking config.yml `rules.kit-defaults` (`Settings.kitDefaults`; a number may be `vanilla`, left out of the map; a missing or invalid value falls back to `KitRule.builtIn`); `Settings.migrate` moves the old `rules.hunger`-style keys there and `KitRegistry.load` turns old `build`/`damage` kit keys into rules, rewriting kits.yml; `Kit.disabled` (kits.yml `disabled: true`, `/duels kit toggle`) makes `Kit.canUse` false for everyone, so every menu, tab completion, queue, request, party fight and hosted event refuses it through that one check; `QueueManager.match` empties its queues and `EventManager.hostScheduled` skips it; custom kits stay enabled when their base kit is off; `Match` keeps `rounds-to-win` from its start; kit rule commands are only `/duels kit rule`; kit effects have an amplifier 0 to `Kit.MAX_AMPLIFIER` (2) and seconds (0 for the whole fight), stored as `effects.<name>.{amplifier,seconds}` (the old `["speed 2"]` list is read and rewritten), whole-fight ones given by `Kit.applyStatus` at the countdown and timed ones by `applyTimedEffects` when the fight starts; `KitAdminMenu`'s effects submenu lists `KitPalette.potionEffects` (the effects of the custom kit potions, in their order; other effects a kit gives come last) and asks amplifier then seconds with `AnvilInput` through the swappable `KitAdminMenu.anvil` (tests answer it, MockBukkit opens no anvil), numbers up to the rule's `max()` (`pearl-cooldown` in seconds, `hits-to-win` counted per victim in `Match.hit`, only for hits outside the victim's invulnerability: Paper fires the damage event again for a harder hit inside it, and for every hit once a knockback-only kit zeroes the damage); `rounds-to-win` makes a duel a series: a knockout in `MatchManager.knockOut` wins the round, `ROUND_OVER` waits `round-delay-seconds`, then `ArenaInstances.resetRound` puts the arena back and `teleportFighters`/`beginCountdown` run again on the same timer; `Match.betweenRounds` turns a quit or `/duel leave` into a loss; combo hit delay and pearl cooldown are undone by `PlayerSnapshot`), `state` (`PlayerSnapshot` saved to the database before a duel touches a player, restored after; leftovers restored at login; a restore deletes the snapshot when it starts the async teleport back, so `SnapshotStore.finishReturn` (called on quit) teleports a player still on the way at once, or they would be saved in the arena), `stats` (wins and streaks in `duels_stats`; Elo ratings per kit in `duels_ratings`, written as deltas, a player's first row in a kit seeded by `INSERT ... SELECT` from the frozen `duels_stats.elo` (the rating before per-kit ratings, kept as `PlayerStats.legacyElo`); the overall rating is the average over existing kits; `Divisions` from `ranked.divisions`; both tables are created in one first transaction because logins read them with `runBlocking` without waiting for `ready()`; `peak` per kit rating, set by `RECORD_RATING` before the rating changes (MySQL applies SET left to right) and added to old tables by `createTables`; `Seasons`: `duels_seasons` archive, one transaction to archive and reset ratings (`DELETE FROM duels_ratings` and `duels_stats.elo` back to the start) and insert the next season's `duels_season_info` row (start, planned end, auto end carried over, name), the season running is one past the last archived; a season without an info row (a database from before it) gets one at start, beginning when the previous one ended (`startKnown` false if none did); `SeasonEnder` ends seasons for `/duels season end confirm` and, from `Duels.tick()`, at the planned end while auto end is on, pays rewards through the public `Rewards`, sends the `seasons.*-warnings` once each (only the shortest passed after a late start) and tells admins on join about a passed plan; `DailyRanked` (in `match`) counts ranked duels per day in the player's PDC; `Bets` (in `match`): stakes taken through `VaultEconomy` when a bet challenge is accepted (`RequestManager.accept`, which holds them for the match or refunds a duel that never started) and stored in `duels_bets`, settled from `MatchManager.onFinish` by `Match.winnerTeams` (no winner refunds), stakes still stored at start refunded from `Bets.tick` once an economy is there, so shutdown only forgets them;
